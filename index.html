About Us

Welcome to Daffodils High School

Established in 1995, Daffodils High School has been a beacon of educational excellence, fostering holistic development and academic achievement. Our commitment to nurturing young minds has made us one of the most respected educational institutions in Hanamkonda.

With state-of-the-art infrastructure, experienced faculty, and a student-centric approach, we ensure that every child receives the best possible education and opportunities to excel.

Principal's Message

"At Daffodils High School, we believe in creating an environment where every child can discover their potential and develop into confident, compassionate, and capable individuals. Our dedicated team of educators works tirelessly to ensure that students not only excel academically but also develop strong character and values."

- Mr Rama Rao Sir

Principal, Daffodils High School

Our Facilities

Academics

Pre-Primary

Playway method, Montessori approach, Activity-based learning for ages 3-5. Focus on social skills, creativity, and foundational learning through fun activities.

Primary (I-V)

Foundation building, Conceptual learning, Co-curricular activities. Strong emphasis on languages, mathematics, and environmental studies with hands-on learning.

Middle (VI-VIII)

Subject specialization, Lab work, Project-based learning. Introduction to sciences, mathematics, social studies with practical experiments and research projects.

Secondary (IX-X)

Board exam preparation, Career counseling, Competitive exam training. Comprehensive coverage of all subjects with focus on exam techniques and future planning.
