About Us

Welcome to Daffodils High School

Founded in 1995, Daffodils High School has been a beacon of educational excellence. We provide a nurturing environment where students develop academically, socially, and emotionally.

Our mission is to empower students with knowledge, skills, and values to become responsible global citizens.
